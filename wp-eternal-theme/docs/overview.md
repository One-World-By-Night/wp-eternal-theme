# WP Eternal Theme Overview

WP Eternal Theme is the look of OWbN's sites: council, chronicles, sso, archivist and support. It gives them the brand colours, the fixed header, the footer and dark mode, and leaves the pages to Elementor.

## What it does

- **Header and footer.** Two Elementor templates the theme creates when it's switched on, see [Header and Footer](help/header-and-footer.md).
- **Brand colours.** OWbN's red, coral, burgundy and greys, ready to use, see [Brand Colours and Dark Mode](help/brand-colors.md).
- **Pages.** Built in Elementor, see [Building Pages](help/building-pages.md).
- **The plugins it needs.** Elementor and WP Dark Mode, offered for install, and Elementor Pro, which you add yourself, see [Required Plugins](help/required-plugins.md).
- **Authors' own work.** On a site with BetterDocs or TablePress, an author sees and edits only their own docs and tables, see [Docs and Tables for Authors](help/author-tools.md).

## Who does what

- **Site admins** switch the theme on, install its plugins, and edit the header and footer.
- **Page editors** build pages in Elementor.

## Where to start

- A new site: [Required Plugins](help/required-plugins.md), then [Header and Footer](help/header-and-footer.md).
- A new page: [Building Pages](help/building-pages.md).
- Something looks wrong: [Theme Troubleshooting](help/troubleshooting.md).

## Getting help on a screen

The theme's screens in wp-admin have a **?** next to their title that opens their help page right there, and every page is also here on support.owbn.net.
